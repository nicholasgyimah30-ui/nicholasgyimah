# Opportunity Pathways

Build me a clean, minimal, and professional personal portfolio website for Nicholas Gyimah — a Ghana-based Talent Ecosystem Builder, Future of Work Advocate, and Student Opportunity Strategist. The portfolio should feel modern, purposeful, and globally credible — reflecting someone doing serious, mission-driven work at the intersection of career development, opportunity access, and the future of work in Africa.

🎨 Design Direction

Style: Clean and minimal — lots of white space, sharp typography, purposeful use of color
Color Palette: Deep navy or charcoal as the primary color, with a warm gold or amber accent (reflecting ambition and African energy), and clean white/off-white backgrounds
Typography: Use a bold modern sans-serif for headings (like Inter, DM Sans, or Satoshi) and a clean readable body font
Feel: Globally professional yet distinctly African — confident, calm, and credible
Layout: Single-page scroll with smooth anchor navigation, fully responsive on mobile


🧭 Navigation / Sections
